# Cockpit commentary updater

1. Save your current working cockpit.
2. Download and extract this ZIP; open `Cockpit_Commentary_Updater.html` in Chrome or Edge.
3. Select your latest saved Budget vs Actual cockpit HTML, including a previously updated file.
4. Click **Download updated cockpit** and open the new file.

Budget vs Actual now leads its unit and site commentary with measured line efficiency and paid-hours performance. Where the source supports it, it explains that the operating allowance may contain an optimistic planning assumption. The hours are calculated from the current submission and selected budget, rather than fixed to a particular weekly number.

Daily, weekly and period commentary, Copy and Excel use the same explanation. Expand **Planning assumptions — supporting hours** for the selected-date reconciliation. ABC's submission comparison appears in ABC and as the ABC component of Site; it is not attributed to units without a separate submission. A changed budget-volume assumption is flagged for a like-for-like review. GOE remains a separate savings target whose delivery needs reconciliation.

Budget, actuals, labour recovery and financial variance calculations are retained. The updater preserves saved data, settings, product amendments and the original file. It also includes the current product register and Bang Bang Quality correction. Use **Save dashboard** for future edits.

This ZIP contains code only, without saved employee, complaint or financial records. All processing takes place on your computer.
